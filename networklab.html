🎯 Lab Philosophy

This laboratory is designed around investigation and evidence. Students do not simply copy commands or program output. Each experiment asks them to observe a network behaviour, collect evidence, explain what happened and submit their own work.

🧭 Your Learning Journey

🐧

Warm-up

Discover Linux network identity, connectivity, routes and active services.

🦈

Use Wireshark to reconstruct real application-layer conversations.

🔌

🖧
